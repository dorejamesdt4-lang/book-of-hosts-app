# Theatre first rehearsal

Open the Theatre and choose **First test · recorded sample**. Wait for three narration clips to be ready.

1. Play the opening; pause and resume partway through. Stop resets the current clip. The mouth follows actual audio and closes when stopped.
2. Let the opening finish. The clue must wait for the host. Next scene moves to the Jester dialogue; press Play. Her dialogue stops at investigation.
3. Continue through investigation, music and the mini-game. These are host-run cues. Nothing advances itself across these stops.
4. Play the reveal and check that curtain call waits. Use Scene navigator to repeat any cue.
5. Open Wardrobe & Player Cards. Dress a narrator, randomise a face, save, and return to Theatre. Revisit the scene to apply the saved appearance.
6. Try mystery script or send a validated mystery from Ruby with Use in Theatre. In Wardrobe, select each guest to see only that character's text in the scroll panel. Download individual player scrolls for another device; they omit other characters and the solution. This remains a host-managed rehearsal, not an authenticated player portal.
7. Download Narrator Package, then upload it again. Ready audio and wardrobe should restore. Unrecorded lines are listed so you can add clips before the show.

The bundled rehearsal contains original synthetic test speech generated with eSpeak 1.48.15, not a production narrator voice. It intentionally covers narration, clue, pause, music and mini-game cues. Portraits are SVG prototypes. Online accounts, live device synchronisation and final illustrated artwork remain future work.
